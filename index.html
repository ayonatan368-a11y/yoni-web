About Yonatan

Building useful digital experiences from idea to launch.

I’m a developer and designer based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, working across web development, software interfaces and visual design. I enjoy turning complicated needs into clean, practical products that people can understand and use without friction.

My Approach: Design with purpose, build with clarity.

My work sits between strategy and implementation. I like to understand the problem deeply, shape the structure of the experience, and then turn that into a product that looks polished and feels intuitive on every screen.

Quality & Execution

Whether the goal is a landing page, an internal platform, or a complete web app, I care about the same things: thoughtful information flow, responsive design, good performance, and clean code that is easy to maintain.
